\section{Runtime and deployment optimisation}

The stable-object segmenter was fine-tuned from COCO-pretrained YOLO26n-seg on
a 21-class Mapillary Vistas taxonomy designed for persistent road landmarks.
The taxonomy includes buildings, walls, fences, signs, poles and street
furniture, and supplies instance masks to the spatial and OCR layers. The
selected checkpoint was exported to a fixed-batch-one, 640-pixel FP16 TensorRT
engine. HumanSLAM explicitly loads the engine as a segmentation task so that
both boxes and masks are retained. The PyTorch checkpoint is preserved as the
portable reference implementation, while the TensorRT engine is the deployed
RTX 4070 implementation.
