\section{Accuracy--latency trade-off}

\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Detector accuracy--latency trade-off. Mask accuracy uses the 2,000
  labelled Mapillary validation images; pipeline latency uses the identical
  240-image cross-domain manifest. Lower latency and higher accuracy are
  preferable.}
  \label{tab:detector-tradeoff}
  \small
  \begin{tabular}{@{}lrrrrrr@{}}
    \toprule
    Model & Mask P & Mask R & mAP50 & mAP50--95 & Median (ms) & P95 (ms) \\
    \midrule
    COCO PyTorch       & --     & --     & --     & --     & 13.01 & 15.37 \\
    Mapillary PyTorch  & 0.3209 & 0.1566 & 0.1366 & 0.0572 & 13.30 & 15.14 \\
    Mapillary TensorRT & 0.3207 & 0.1578 & 0.1393 & 0.0582 & \textbf{4.84} & \textbf{6.02} \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

COCO accuracy is omitted because its labels do not match the HumanSLAM
taxonomy. TensorRT preserved Mapillary mask accuracy while reducing median
pipeline latency by 63.6\% (2.75-fold); full-validation inference also fell
from 10.93 to 2.27 ms per image. It was therefore selected for its stable
landmark classes, masks and substantially lower latency.
